# Privacy overview

Signed-in libraries and account preferences are stored in protected account storage. Signing out does not delete your cloud records. Guest collections, recent searches and some preferences use local device storage; clearing browser/app data can remove unsynced guest collections.

Autoplay learning is kept separately per account on the device, with feedback and recent recommendation history retained for up to 30 days and 7 days respectively. Settings provides **Reset autoplay learning**. Clearing listening history also clears local autoplay learning.

Search, imports, artwork and playback contact supported providers. Those services receive relevant requests and network information such as an IP address; embedded players may use cookies under their own policies. Provider availability, retention and advertising rules remain controlled by those providers.

This repository includes public product information, sample screenshots and the creator-supplied promo film. It does not include private libraries, account exports, application source, Firebase configuration or signing material.

Do not post private account information in community discussions. Report suspected exposure through [private security reporting](SECURITY.md).

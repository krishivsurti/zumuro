# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Discuss behaviour and ideas without harassment, personal attacks or discrimination.

Protect privacy. Do not post another person's account information, private playlists, credentials or identifying screenshots. Use private reporting for security issues.

Respect content ownership and provider rules. Do not submit changes designed to steal credentials, expose private libraries or bypass access controls.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions and restrict participation when needed. Contact the maintainer through GitHub for community concerns; security reports should follow SECURITY.md.
